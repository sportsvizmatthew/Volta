Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

In short: Volta doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your practice stays on your devices, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

Who we are

Volta is made by Matthew Parry, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: volta_support@icloud.com.

What Volta stores

Volta saves what you create while practising: the songs you've opened, loops, sections, speeds, speed trainer history, tabs and setlists. This is stored on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

iCloud sync

With Volta Pro, you can sync your practice between your devices. Sync uses Apple's iCloud and stores your data in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for Volta at any time in your device's iCloud settings.

Apple Music

Volta asks for access to Apple Music so it can search for and play songs, and show your library. Playback and search go directly between your device and Apple. Volta doesn't send your listening or your library anywhere else.

Purchases

Volta Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer receives no payment details or personal information, only Apple's sales reports, which don't identify you.

Share to Volta

When you share a web page to Volta from Safari, Volta reads the text of that page only, to keep the tab it contains. It doesn't read any other pages or your browsing history.

System audio on Mac

On Mac, if you set the music volume below 100%, Volta asks for permission to record system audio. It uses this only to replay its own Apple Music playback at the volume you choose. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and no other app's audio is used.

Ratings

Volta may occasionally ask you to rate it, using Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles your response.

Children

Volta doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
