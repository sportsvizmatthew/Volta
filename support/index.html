Common questions

Do I need Apple Music?

Yes. Volta plays songs from your Apple Music library and the Apple Music catalogue, so it needs an active Apple Music subscription. When Volta first opens, allow it access to Apple Music.

What's free, and what does Volta Pro add?

Looping and speed changes are free on every song. Sections, tabs and the speed trainer are free on 3 songs. Volta Pro is a one-off purchase, not a subscription: it unlocks unlimited songs, setlists and iCloud sync across your devices. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I restore Volta Pro on a new device?

Open Settings in Volta and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Volta Pro works on iPhone, iPad and Mac from one purchase.

My songs and sections aren't syncing

iCloud sync is part of Volta Pro. Check that you're signed in to iCloud on each device. If you've just bought Pro, sync starts the next time you leave Volta, so switch to another app and back. Settings in Volta shows whether sync is on.

Does slowing down change the pitch?

No. Anywhere from 50% to 120%, the song stays in the same key.

How do I use a foot pedal?

Pair a Bluetooth page-turner pedal with your device as you would a keyboard. Volta responds to the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, and the space bar. Go to Settings › Test your pedal to check what each switch does.

How do I add a tab I found on the web?

In Safari, tap Share and choose Volta. Volta keeps the tab and adds it to the matching song. You can also copy a tab and paste it into the tab panel on the practice screen.

Why does Volta on Mac ask to record system audio?

Apple Music doesn't let apps set their own volume on Mac, so Volta lowers the music itself by passing its own playback through at the level you choose. It only asks when you set the music volume below 100%, nothing is recorded or saved, and no other app's audio is touched.

A song won't play

Check that the song plays in the Music app and that you're online. Some songs aren't available in every country, and songs added to your library from elsewhere may not be playable through Apple Music.
