Volta
Guitar practice with Apple Music

Learn the hard parts, at your own speed.

Volta loops, slows down and speed-trains the songs already in your Apple Music library. Nothing to import.

Loop any section

Tap Set A and Set B while the song plays to mark a riff or solo, fine-tune each end, and save it as a named section.

Slow it down, or speed it up

Anywhere from 50% to 120% speed without changing the pitch.

Build up to speed

The speed trainer starts slow and steps the tempo up each time the loop repeats, and keeps your best speed for every section.

Tabs, setlists and a pedal

Keep tab beside the music, group songs into setlists, and control it all with a Bluetooth page-turner pedal. On iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Free to try. Looping and speed changes are free on every song; sections, tabs and the speed trainer are free on 3 songs. Volta Pro is a single purchase, not a subscription. Requires an Apple Music subscription.
